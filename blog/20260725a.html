Green lines indicate mods only, blue lines indicate both/either resource packs and/or mods and proper names are indicated by italic lines.

Ethical to use

Completely ethical

These mods/resource packs are totally fine to use as they are purely aesthetic and don't change the gameplay.

  • Higher resolution texture like Faithful resource packs.
  • Different art styles such as one that makes the game looks more realistic.
  • 3D models for blocks and items.
  • Different fonts.
  • Totems' appearance.
  • Most options for resource packs on Vanilla Tweaks.
  • Most performance and optimisation mods, examples include Sodium and FerriteCore.
  • Shader mods, Iris for instance.

Accepted

Whilst these mods/resource packs slightly make certain tasks easier, they are still considered ethical to use by most players.

  • Zoom mods.
    • OptiFine why.
  • Fullbright and unobtrusive texture.
  • Improving visibility of blocks, items, etc. and some of their states, such as
    • Making ores, suspicious sand and gravel, fully aged crops, amethyst clusters, strings, etc. easier to spot;
    • Making melon and pumpkin stems distinguishable;
    • Visualising directions, stages, redstone levels, growth, etc. of functional and non-functional blocks;
    • Making hearts more visible when under the Wither effect.

Grey area

Debatable

Mods/Resource packs in this section might be subjected to debate on whether they provide unfair advantages to players or not.

  • Schematic mods, among them is Litematica might be controversial.

Questionable

These mods/resource packs DO provide somewhat unfair advantages to players in specific aspects. I will avoid using these mods/resource packs in my Hardcore world(s), and you guys should do so too!

  • Xaero's Minimap and other minimap mods.
  • Health indicators.
  • Waypoints that display the location of places.
  • Making mob and players easier to spot.
    • Highlighting mobs and players that are under the Invisibility effect.

Unethical to use

Semi-anarchy

Usage of these mods/resource packs is generally considered as cheating, though they don't significantly alter how players will play the game.

  • X-ray.
  • ESP.
  • Revealing location of every other player.

Pure anarchy

These mods/resource packs break all Vanilla gameplay for essentially altering Minecraft's mechanics, giving players huge advantages they aren't supposed to have and/or changing how you play the game far from how you should normally do it.

  • Altering player's movement such as those that allow climbing when not on ladders or vines, walking on water, flying, etc.
  • Automating actions, from mining, building, to fighting.
  • Freecam.
